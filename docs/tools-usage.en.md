The single catalog is `src/farm-support/resource-catalog.js`; independent raw-byte approvals are in `resource-approval.js`. The 19-item review is `tool-resource-review.json`. Technical scans flag review needs and never approve resources. Originals remain unchanged, including metadata. No automatic translations or invented translated downloads are provided. Chinese downloads remain labeled Chinese originals on the English page.

This round includes ten items: a blank friction-planning XLSX, two framework PDFs and seven local calendar, health-guide, navigation and transit PNGs. Frameworks and historical illustrations are research/service-design examples, not clinical diagnostic tools or validated causal/medical-outcome evidence. Verify local addresses, phone numbers, transit, farming patterns and service suitability before use; medical content needs professional review. The original Chinese emergency number 120 is not global; use local emergency services. Keep printed materials and human assistance available.

Nine candidates are withheld: three DOCX originals and the replication PPTX received structural body/property/note review, but an original-file renderer is unavailable on this host, so layout/embedded-image visual checks remain incomplete. The Journey Maps overview and A–D contain interview-quotation identifiers or references whose reuse basis remains unconfirmed. They were not copied, sanitized or made downloadable. Provide a reviewed public version or complete the outstanding checks. A public URL alone is not an open license.

Never publish completed surveys, interview records, assessment sheets, personnel CSVs, databases or backups. The existing CSVs are header-only templates, not upload-to-database files. Tools do not replace consent, local verification or professional judgment, and do not automatically establish project outcomes.
